 Audi Q2  – Premium Compact SUV 
 The Audi Q2 in elegant white finish delivers a perfect blend of luxury, sporty styling, and everyday practicality. As a premium compact SUV, it is designed for urban driving while offering advanced technology, efficient performance, and Audi’s signature build quality. 
 With its bold front grille, sharp LED headlights, and dynamic body lines, the Q2 stands out on the road. Inside, the cabin features a modern digital cockpit, high-quality materials, and intuitive infotainment, ensuring a comfortable and connected driving experience.

 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 
 1. Is the Audi Q2 a good family car? 
 Yes, it comfortably seats 4–5 passengers and offers enough cargo space for daily family use, though it’s more suited for small families or urban lifestyles. 
 2. What engine options are available? 
 The Q2 comes with multiple petrol engines ranging from 1.0L to 2.0L TFSI, offering a balance between performance and fuel efficiency. 
 3. Does the Audi Q2 have all-wheel drive? 
 Yes, selected variants come with Audi’s Quattro all-wheel drive system for improved traction and handling. 
 4. What is the fuel economy of the Audi Q2? 
 It averages around 5.2L/100km , making it efficient for a luxury compact SUV. 
 5. Is the Audi Q2 good for city driving? 
 Absolutely. Its compact dimensions and responsive steering make it ideal for urban roads and tight parking spaces. 
 6. What technology features are included? 
 It includes a digital cockpit, touchscreen infotainment, smartphone connectivity, voice control, and advanced driver assistance systems. 
 7. How much boot space does it offer? 
 The Q2 offers around 405 liters , expandable to over 1000 liters with rear seats folded. 
 8. Is the white color a good choice? 
 Yes, white enhances the Q2’s sporty design, is easier to maintain in hot climates, and has strong resale appeal.
