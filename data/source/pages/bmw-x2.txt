 BMW X2 – Sporty Compact Luxury SUV 
 The BMW X2 is a bold and sporty compact luxury SUV that blends coupe-like styling with everyday practicality. Redesigned with a more aggressive stance, sharp lines, and a sloping roofline, the X2 stands out as one of the most stylish options in its segment. 
 Built for drivers who want performance and individuality, the X2 delivers strong turbocharged power, advanced all-wheel-drive capability, and a tech-focused interior. It combines BMW’s signature driving dynamics with modern comfort, making it ideal for both city driving and long-distance travel. 
 Key Features & Specifications 
 Body Type: Compact luxury crossover SUV 
 Engine Options: 
 2.0L Turbocharged Inline-4 (xDrive28i) 
 2.0L Turbocharged High-Performance (M35i) 
 Power Output: 
 241 HP (standard) 
 Up to 312 HP (M35i variant) 
 Transmission: 7-speed dual-clutch automatic 
 Drivetrain: xDrive All-Wheel Drive (AWD) 
 0–100 km/h: ~6.2 sec (standard) / ~5.2 sec (M35i) 
 Fuel Economy: ~28 MPG combined 
 Dimensions & Capacity 
 Seating Capacity: 5 passengers 
 Cargo Space: ~25.3 cu.ft. (expandable to ~51.7 cu.ft.) 
 Length: ~179.3 inches 
 Wheelbase: ~106 inches

 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 
 1. Is the BMW X2 a good SUV? 
 Yes, it offers a strong mix of performance, style, and technology, making it a great choice in the compact luxury SUV segment. 
 2. What’s the difference between xDrive28i and M35i? 
 The xDrive28i focuses on balanced performance (241 HP), while the M35i is a high-performance version with up to 312 HP and sportier tuning. 
 3. Is the BMW X2 fuel efficient? 
 Yes, it delivers around 28 MPG combined , which is good for a luxury compact SUV. 
 4. Does the BMW X2 come with AWD? 
 Yes, all variants come with BMW’s xDrive all-wheel-drive system as standard. 
 5. Is it bigger than the BMW X1? 
 The X2 is slightly more stylish and coupe-like, but offers a bit less rear headroom and cargo space compared to the X1. 
 6. What technology features are included? 
 It includes a digital cockpit, curved display, smartphone connectivity, navigation, and advanced driver assistance systems. 
 7. Is the BMW X2 good for families? 
 It’s suitable for small families, but those needing more space may prefer larger SUVs like the X3. 
 8. What makes the BMW X2 unique? 
 Its coupe-style design , sporty driving feel, and aggressive styling set it apart from traditional compact SUVs.
