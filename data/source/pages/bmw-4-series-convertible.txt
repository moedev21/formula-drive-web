 BMW 4 Series Convertible – Luxury Open-Top Performance 
 The BMW 4 Series Convertible combines dynamic performance with premium luxury, delivering an exhilarating open-air driving experience. Designed with BMW’s signature sporty aesthetics, it features a sleek body, aggressive front grille, and a refined soft-top roof that opens in seconds for a true convertible feel. 
 Powered by advanced turbocharged engines with mild hybrid technology, the 4 Series Convertible offers a perfect balance of efficiency and performance. Inside, the cabin is crafted with high-end materials, a fully digital cockpit, and BMW’s latest infotainment system, making every drive both comfortable and connected. 
 Key Features & Specifications

 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 
 1. Is the BMW 4 Series Convertible a good daily driver? 
 Yes, it offers a comfortable ride, modern tech, and decent fuel efficiency, making it practical for everyday use despite being a convertible. 
 2. Does it come with a hardtop or soft top? 
 The model uses a soft-top roof , which is lighter and improves performance and efficiency. 
 3. How fast is the BMW 4 Series Convertible ? 
 It can accelerate from 0–100 km/h in around 6 seconds , depending on the variant. 
 4. Is the rear seat usable? 
 Yes, but it’s best suited for short trips or smaller passengers, as space is limited compared to sedans. 
 5. What’s new in the model? 
 It features mild hybrid technology, updated lighting design, and the latest iDrive 8.5 infotainment system . 
 6. Is it fuel efficient? 
 Yes, for a luxury convertible, it offers around 30 MPG combined , which is quite efficient. 
 7. Does it support Apple CarPlay and Android Auto? 
 Yes, both are available wirelessly as standard features. 
 8. Is it worth buying over a coupe? 
 If you enjoy open-air driving and style, the convertible is worth it. However, coupes offer slightly better practicality and rigidity.
