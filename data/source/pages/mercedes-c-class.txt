 Mercedes C-200 – Luxury Meets Intelligent Performance 
 The  Mercedes C-200 is a refined luxury sedan that blends elegance, comfort, and advanced technology. As part of the latest C-Class generation, it delivers a premium driving experience with a focus on smooth performance, cutting-edge features, and modern design. 
 With its sleek exterior styling, signature LED lighting, and sophisticated interior, the C-200 is designed for drivers who appreciate both comfort and class. Equipped with a mild-hybrid powertrain and Mercedes’ advanced MBUX infotainment system, it offers efficiency without compromising performance.

 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 
 1. Is the Mercedes C-200 a good luxury car? 
 Yes, it offers a perfect mix of comfort, technology, and efficiency, making it one of the best entry-level luxury sedans. 
 2. What engine does the C-200 have? 
 It comes with a 1.5L turbocharged engine with a 48V mild-hybrid system , delivering around 204 HP. 
 3. Is the Mercedes C-200 fuel efficient? 
 Yes, it averages around 6–7 L/100 km , which is efficient for a luxury sedan. 
 4. Does it come with all-wheel drive? 
 Yes, some variants offer 4MATIC all-wheel drive , while the standard model is rear-wheel drive. 
 5. Is the C-200 fast? 
 It’s not a performance-focused model but delivers smooth acceleration with 0–100 km/h in about 7.3 seconds . 
 6. What technology features are included? 
 It includes MBUX infotainment, digital displays, voice control, smartphone connectivity, and advanced driver assistance systems. 
 7. How spacious is the interior? 
 It comfortably seats 5 passengers with a 455L boot , making it practical for daily and long trips. 
 8. Who should buy the Mercedes C-200? 
 It’s ideal for buyers looking for luxury, comfort, and technology rather than high-performance driving.
